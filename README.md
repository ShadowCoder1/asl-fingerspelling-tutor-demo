# ASL fingerspelling tutor: 3-letter demo

Live: https://shadowcoder1.github.io/asl-fingerspelling-tutor-demo/

A short demo of the study task: three letters (B, L, F), baseline once each,
training three times each, test once each (15 trials). No consent form or
questions, and nothing is saved: hand tracking runs in the browser and no
video or landmarks leave the page.

This teaches fingerspelling handshapes, not ASL. Grading is approximate and
can be wrong.
